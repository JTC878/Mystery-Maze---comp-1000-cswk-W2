NOTE: If you get stuck on a black screen after completing depth 1 press enter!!


In this game you will enter a dungeon where each level is a randomly generated maze called "depths", you are recommended
to start from depth 1 but you can input any depth between 1 and 10. Depth 1 is recommended so that you can collect items before getting 
to the harder levels. 
Once you start the game, you will be positioned in the middle of the maze and will try to make your way out of it, to do this you first 
will need to collect the golden key which will be randomly generated at any point opposite of the exit door. Once you collect the golden
key just step through the exit door labelled 'D'.
Once you manage to exit you will delve deeper into the dungeon and start the next depth. This means the maze will be bigger, have more
enemies, enemies will be faster and will begin to chase you from further away, more items will be generated(in a random range),
and possibly more doors too.   
Of course the best way to make it to a high depth is to explore the maze thouroughly and collect items. Doors will be also generated be generated
at random points in the maze which will require you to unlock it with a key or lockpick to advance along that path. Be careful with lockpicking though. 


Keybinds:
W- Move up 
A- Move left 
S- Move down 
D- Move right
Spacebar- Wait a turn 
X- Attempt lockpick of regular doors you're standing next to
1- Use Slow orb
2- Use Jump orb
3- Use Tele orb
4- Use Kill orb
5- Use Super Tele orb
6- Use Key while standing next to a regular door

To use the golden key just step through the exit door on the outside of the maze labelled 'D'


Items and Mechanics:

Slow Orb - Slow enemies, useful in the deeper depths. Try to always keep enemy speed at 1 or below.

Jump Orb - Jump in a straight path either over enemies or walls if you stand next to them, these are extremely helpful lifesavers to get away while stuck in a dead end. 
Direction of the jump is determined by the last movement key pressed.

Tele Orb - Teleport to a random item in the maze, there's no safety precautions with these. Test your luck you might just end up teleporting to a space an enemy is on, or a golden key who knows. 
If there are no items left in the maze you will teleport to the exit door

Kill Orb - Kills the nearest enemy, very menacing name but also incredibly valuable. Use these sparingly so you can save them for dire situations or deeper depths...

Super Tele Orb - A super item. Very rare and also one of the best items to have. Compared to Tele Orbs, these have a safety precaution built in and will never teleport you on top of an enemy. 
Teleports you to the golden key if you don't have it, if you already do then it will teleport you to the exit door. Almost like a skip button

Key - Regular rusted old keys that have been scattered throughout the depths, luckily they can unlock any door except for the exit door. Just stand next to one and press the key. Hah.

Golden Key - This is your ticket out of here, this unlocks the exit door to leave the current level, don't need to press anything just step through the door and it'll unlock!! 
Look for the 'D' at the edge of the maze, you can't miss it.

Lockpicking - Since you're such a skilled and prepared adventurer you brought a magic talking lockpick with you, however it doesn't seem to like you very much, 
every time you ask for help it asks you to solve one of its riddles first. Be suspicious of this guy.
