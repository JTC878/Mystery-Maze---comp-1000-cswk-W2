In this game you will enter a dungeon where each level is a randomly generated maze called "depths", you start from depth 1. In this maze you will start in the middle and will try to make your way out of it, once you reach the exit you will delve deeper into the
dungeon and another maze will be generated as the next "depth". Of course the best way to make it to a high depth is to explore the maze thouroughly and collect items. Puzzles will be required to be solved to unlock additional paths within the maze which will hold more 
powerful equipment. Example inventory: Sword - Armour - Helmet - Boots - Minor Potion of HP. Once you die you start from the beginning. 


Further ideas: Each level enemies speed up exponentionally, to make this easier for yourself to progress you must collect slow orbs which will spawn randomly around the maze, these will make the more difficult levels easier as enemies will take longer to catch up to you. Teleportation tiles. Fog of war.
